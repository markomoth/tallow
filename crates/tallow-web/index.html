Something under the church has taken the candle. A roguelike for the keyboard, about an hour a run.

Your run and journal are kept in this browser. Clearing its site data clears them. Play in your terminal Source

All keys

arrows or hjklMove. Walk into things to fight, open, ring, light.
yubnMove diagonally
Shift + directionRun
oExplore until something new is in view
. and RWait a turn; rest until healed
c and CSnuff or light your candle; shut the doors beside you
g and iPick up; open your pack
t and fThrow; fire a sling or crossbow
sStudy or render the body underfoot
zRites: arrows to read one, a letter or Enter to cast
S + directionShove a creature back
GGuard: wait braced and answer every miss
> and <Down the stairs; up, once you carry the Vigil Candle
FFlare the Vigil Candle, on the way back up
xLook at a creature; Tab moves between them
@ M ?Your character, the journal, help
qBack to the start menu; the run is kept
